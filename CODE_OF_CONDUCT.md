# Code of conduct

The hayatepy community is committed to a welcoming, respectful, and
harassment-free environment.

Be constructive, assume good intent, focus criticism on technical work, and
respect differences in experience and background. Harassment, personal
attacks, discrimination, and publication of private information are not
acceptable.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation when
behavior threatens the safety or effectiveness of the community. Report
conduct concerns privately to the organization owner through GitHub.

This policy follows the principles of the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
